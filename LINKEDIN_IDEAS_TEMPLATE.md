# LinkedIn Post Ideas — Weekly Generator

## Sources to scan (check all)

1. **Lenny's Newsletter** — https://www.lennysnewsletter.com — scan recent post titles. Topics he covers become what everyone talks about.
2. **a16z AI** — https://a16z.com/ai/ — check "Latest" section. VC perspective on where AI products are heading.
3. **Hugging Face Daily Papers** — https://huggingface.co/papers — trending AI research. Look for product-relevant papers (agents, LLMs, evaluation).
4. **OpenAI Blog** — https://openai.com/blog/ — new capabilities create product opportunities.
5. **Anthropic Blog** — https://www.anthropic.com/news — Claude releases, safety research.

## How to generate ideas

1. Browse each source and collect 10-15 trending topics
2. Cross-reference with George's experience:
   - AI/GenAI product delivery (agentic contract parsing, LLM agents, model evaluation 61%→91%)
   - Healthcare IT (13M patients EHR, HL7 FHIR, HIPAA)
   - Enterprise platforms (9M daily customers, 8000 stores)
   - Job hunting pipeline (14-rule prompt template, quality gates, agent orchestration)
   - Regulated environments (healthcare, finance, compliance)
3. Select 5 topics where George has a real story to tell
4. For each idea, write: the hook (why it's trending), George's angle (what he's done that connects), and a one-line pitch for the post

## Output format

```
📝 LinkedIn Post Ideas — {DATE}

1. {TITLE}
   Trending because: {why it's hot}
   Your angle: {what you've done that connects}
   Pitch: {one-line idea for the post}

2. {TITLE}
   ...

(5 total)

💡 Tip: Pick one, draft it, post within 48 hours while the trend is fresh.
```

## Rules
- Ideas must connect to REAL experience — no generic "here's my take on AI" posts
- No "I'm thrilled to announce" energy
- Keep pitches concrete — specific stories, specific lessons
- If a topic is trending on multiple sources, prioritize it
- Don't repeat ideas from previous weeks (check existing files)
